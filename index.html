Personal Drive Backup

A personal, non-commercial utility built and used by Mitch Isler.

What it does

Personal Drive Backup saves copies of my own personal data as files in my own Google Drive. It uses Google Sign-In to get permission to create and update its backup files in Drive. It does not read, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive.

How Google data is used

  • The app requests access only to files it creates in Google Drive.
  • Data is written to the signed-in user's own Drive and is not sent to any other server or third party.
  • The app is not sold, does not show ads, and is not intended for public use.
