Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy describes how Personal Drive Backup ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Mitch Isler.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests permission to create and manage files that it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It may also receive your basic Google account profile (name and email address) to identify which account is signed in.

How information is used

The app uses this access only to write and update backup files in your own Google Drive. It does not access other files in your Drive, and it does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to creating backups.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored in your Google Drive. The app does not copy your Google data to any other server, and it does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. OAuth tokens, if stored, are kept only on the device running the app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Backup files the app created remain in your Drive until you delete them, which you can do directly in Google Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
